CaloScan – Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 15, 2026

CaloScan ("we", "our", or "the app") is a calorie and nutrition tracking app for iPhone. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

CaloScan stores all data locally on your device. We do not operate servers, create user accounts, or collect personal data.

  • Food & meal entries – stored locally using SwiftData
  • Recipes & ingredients – stored locally on your device
  • Health data – read from Apple Health (active calories) and written to Apple Health (meals), only with your explicit permission. This data never leaves your device through our app.
  • Camera & photos – used only to photograph food for recognition. Images are processed on-device using Apple's Vision framework and are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Settings & goals – stored locally using UserDefaults

2. Third-Party Services

CaloScan queries the following external databases to retrieve nutritional information:

  • USDA FoodData Central – fdc.nal.usda.gov – U.S. Department of Agriculture food database (no personal data sent)
  • Open Food Facts – openfoodfacts.org – open food product database (no personal data sent)

Only your search terms (food names or barcodes) are sent to these services. No personal information is transmitted.

3. Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, CaloScan can:

  • Read active energy burned to calculate your net calorie balance
  • Write meal nutrition data (calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat)

Health data is never shared with third parties, uploaded to servers, or used for advertising.

4. Camera & Photo Library

Camera access is used to photograph food items for on-device recognition using Apple's Vision framework. Photos are not stored permanently unless you explicitly save a recipe photo. No images are uploaded to external servers.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

All data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all data at any time by removing the app from your iPhone. There is no account to delete.

6. Children's Privacy

CaloScan is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
